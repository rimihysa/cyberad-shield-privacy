CyberAd Shield (com.aistudio.cyberadshield.adblk)

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CyberAd Shield ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and designed CyberAd Shield to operate without user accounts or personal data collection on our own servers.

1. Local DNS Filtering & Android VpnService

CyberAd Shield uses Android's built-in VpnService strictly to create a local, device-level DNS filter:

  • Only DNS resolution requests (UDP port 53) are processed locally and forwarded to the DNS configuration you select in the App to block advertisements, trackers, and malicious domains.
  • The App does not act as a remote VPN proxy, does not tunnel or inspect your general web traffic or browsing content, and does not log, store, or sell your browsing history.
  • Local DNS filtering automatically pauses while CyberAd Shield itself is open in the foreground so in-app advertisements can load properly, and re-engages when you exit the App (when protection is enabled).
  • If you optionally choose to enable System Private DNS via ADB permissions (WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS), the App updates only your device's Private DNS hostname setting according to your chosen configuration.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

CyberAd Shield does not require registration or login. App settings—such as your selected DNS configuration, shield status, local session counters, and Firebase notification token—are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences. You can erase this local data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Services Used in the App

While we do not operate personal data servers, the App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may process technical identifiers as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display Banner and App Open ads. AdMob may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications): Used to deliver optional security updates and announcements. Firebase generates a device push registration token and subscribes to general broadcast topics (e.g., all_users). On Android 13+, notifications require your explicit permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), which you can revoke anytime in Android Settings. Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Your Privacy Controls & Choices

  • Reset or Limit Ad Tracking: You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Notification Controls: You can disable push notifications for CyberAd Shield at any time in Android App Settings → Notifications.
  • Local Data Removal: Uninstalling CyberAd Shield or clearing app storage removes all locally saved preferences.

5. Children's Privacy

CyberAd Shield is a general-audience network utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
